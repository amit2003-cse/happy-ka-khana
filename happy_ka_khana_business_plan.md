## 📈 4. Monthly Profit Forecast (50 Fixed Customers)

Agar hamare paas **50 fixed monthly subscription active users** hain (Lunch + Dinner combo):

### Scenario A: Combo Pack Model (₹4,500/Month Plan)
- **Total Thalis / Month**: 3,000 Thalis (50 customers × 2 meals × 30 days)

| Particulars | Calculation | Amount |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Total Monthly Revenue** | 50 customers × ₹4,500 | **₹2,25,000** |
| **Raw Food & Packaging Cost** | 3,000 Thalis × ₹43.00 average | -₹1,29,000 |
| **Delivery Boy Salary & Fuel** | 2 delivery routes (Salary + Fuel) | -₹15,000 |
| **Helper Salary & Utilities** | Commercial Gas, Electricity & helper | -₹20,000 |
| **TOTAL NET MONTHLY PROFIT (Bachat)** | **₹2,25,000 - ₹1,64,000** | **~₹61,000 / Month** |

### Scenario B: Custom Daily Plan Model (₹4,470/Month Plan)
- **Total Monthly Revenue**: 50 customers × ₹4,470 = **₹2,23,500**
- **Raw Food & Packaging Cost**: 3,000 Thalis × ₹45.50 = -₹1,36,500
- **Delivery Boy Salary + Fuel**: -₹16,000
- **Helper Salary + Kitchen Utilities**: -₹18,000
- **TOTAL NET MONTHLY PROFIT (Bachat)**: **~₹53,000 to ₹58,000 / Month**

> 📝 **Summary**: 50 monthly subscription customers banane par saare kharche nikalne ke baad aapki pure monthly bachat **₹53,000 se ₹61,000/Month** rahegi.

## 🛠️ 5. Business Setup & Pre-Launch Checklist

Launch se pehle niche diye gaye operational parameters set karna compulsory hai:

1. **Digital Accounts Setup**:
   - WhatsApp Business App setup karein (Catalog pricing aur Instant Auto-replies configure karein).
   - Bank accounts linked GooglePay / PhonePe Merchant QR code print karein.
2. **FSSAI Registration**:
   - Basic FSSAI food hygiene license online apply karein ([foscos.fssai.gov.in](https://foscos.fssai.gov.in)) — approx cost ₹100/Year.
3. **Stock Procurement**:
   - Heavy 5-Compartment boxes, foil packaging rolls, insulated thali delivery bags, plastic spoons, and small paper cups.
4. **Operational Cut-offs**:
   - **Lunch Order / Cancellation Cut-off**: Morning **9:30 AM** (Delivery: 12:30 PM - 2:00 PM)
   - **Dinner Order / Cancellation Cut-off**: Evening **4:30 PM** (Delivery: 7:30 PM - 9:00 PM)
5. **Advance Payment Policy**:
   - Monthly subscribers se advance monthly subscription payment **1st se 5th date** ke bich collect karein.
6. **Marketing Campaign**:
   - 500-1000 Pamphlets distribution in local PGs, Hostels, and Offices.
   - Google Maps listing setup for local discoverability.
